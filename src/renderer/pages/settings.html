Settings

Layout

Where and how Sillon shows up on the desktop.

The scene only shows "On the wallpaper": in the other positions, Sillon shows the strip.

Scene

Disc, lyrics, big title, cover, "Now playing", buttons, "Up next", clock, bars: place each element by hand, change its size, show or hide it.

Disc

The disc colors are picked in the Colors tab.

Visualizer

For the strip, the full screen and the scene's "Bars" element. Previews animated on a sample rhythm: click to choose.

Display

Colors

The visualizer's color set. "Wallpaper" and "Cover" follow the current image.

Custom color

Pick a hue on the wheel (brighter toward the edge), then the lightness. It becomes the visualizer's color set; for the scene's disc, choose "Custom" below. With the keyboard: left and right arrows for the hue, up and down for the saturation (Shift: faster).

Colors of the scene's disc

Sound

What Sillon listens to, and how sensitively.

"Only the player" ignores voice chat (Discord, TeamSpeak), notifications and games. Recent Windows 10 or 11; otherwise Sillon listens to the whole PC.

Behavior

When Sillon steps back, and how it moves.

Removes the background drift, the ripples and the pulsing to the bass. Sillon also follows the Windows "Animation effects" setting.

Keyboard shortcuts

Spotify account

Optional: shows the upcoming tracks ("Up next" element) and turns on the shuffle and repeat buttons. Sign in with your Spotify account.

The Spotify app built into Sillon only accepts 5 accounts (Spotify's rule), added by its owner. If your account is not one of them, check the option above and create your own app.

Create a free app on the (your account must be Premium), check "Web API" and add this Redirect URI:

http://127.0.0.1:43821/callback

Diagnostics

If something goes wrong: first click the buttons that do not work, then copy the diagnostics (here, or from the Sillon icon's menu after Windows + D to clear the desktop) and paste them to the person helping you. They contain no password or token.
